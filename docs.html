Every call is a coin.

What call is, exactly what pays whom, and the readers this site runs on. Nothing on the site is typed in by hand — every number comes from X, Yahoo Finance, Dexscreener or Blockscout at load time.

The rule

  1. A call is a creator on X, an asset and a direction — with their own post as the receipt when they made the call themselves. The first Pons V2 launch on a call, with the call vault as creatorFeeRecipient, is its coin.
  2. Every trade pays the Pons 1% fee. 70% is the creator side; on a call coin that is the vault (0.5% of volume) and the launcher (0.2%). 30% (0.3%) goes to Pons.
  3. A call is scored as the asset's price now versus its price when the call went on the board (or the close on the day of the post, when a post is the receipt), read live — Coinbase for crypto, Yahoo / Stooq for stocks, Dexscreener for on-chain tokens — sign flipped for shorts.
  4. On every hour (UTC) the vault pays the holders of the call with the largest gain in its direction since it went on the board — pro rata by balance at the snapshot, in USDG. The caller's X handle can claim the launcher share with the claim code below.
  5. The caller can claim the launcher's share of their own call any time by replying to the post with their claim code (below). Until then it accrues to the vault.

The money, in one breath

$100,000 of trading on a call coin in a day = $1,000 of fees = $500 to the vault, $200 to the launcher, $300 to Pons. The vault only ever holds fee income; it never buys or sells anything.

feeIncome24h   = volume.h24 × 0.007        # creator side of the 1% fee
vaultIncome24h = volume.h24 × 0.005
sinceCall      = (priceNow ÷ priceAtCall − 1) × (long ? +1 : −1)

Launching

Paste the post on the site, check the asset and direction, copy the description, go to ponsvault.com/launch, set the creator fee recipient to the vault address, launch. Pons gives the launcher no allocation — if you want a reserve, buy on the curve in the launch transaction. Then paste the CA in "Already launched?"; verified coins are added to calls.json by hand within the hour.

Claim code

Deterministic, so anyone can check it. FNV-1a over the caller's handle (lowercase) and the coin address, base36, upper, 7 characters.

function claimCode(handle, ca){
  let h = 0x811c9dc5 >>> 0;
  for (const ch of (handle.toLowerCase() + ':' + ca.toLowerCase())) { h ^= ch.charCodeAt(0); h = Math.imul(h, 0x01000193) >>> 0; }
  return h.toString(36).toUpperCase().padStart(7, '0').slice(-7);
}

Registry

calls.json — one entry per call: caller, asset, side, at (unix time the call went on the board), atPrice (the live price read at that moment, with atSrc), optional post receipt, ca once verified. creators.json — the handles on the wall; everything about them is read live.

{ "caller": "blknoiz06", "asset": "TSLA", "side": "long", "at": 1757850000, "atPrice": 412.5, "atSrc": "stooq", "post": "", "ca": "" }

Not on chain yet


The vault contract is not deployed. Until it is, the fee recipient on any call coin is a plain wallet published here the day it exists, with every payout tx listed under it. Nothing on this page claims otherwise.
