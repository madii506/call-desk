Questions, answered.

What is a call?

A creator, an asset and a direction. "@blknoiz06 · $TSLA · long." If they posted it themselves, paste the post and it's the receipt; if not, you're giving them the call.

How is "since call" computed?

The asset's price when the call went on the board versus its price right now, read live from the market (Coinbase for crypto, Yahoo / Stooq for stocks, Dexscreener for on-chain tokens). Sign flipped for shorts.

Who gets paid, and when?

On every hour, UTC. The holders of the call coin with the largest gain in its direction split the vault pro rata. The caller's X handle can claim their share any time with their claim code.

Does the creator have to agree?

No. It's a coin about a public call. They can claim their cut, they can't stop it, and they profit from being right.

Where does the money come from?

Only from the 1% trade fee on Pons. 0.5% to the vault, 0.2% to the launcher, 0.3% to Pons. No emissions, no treasury, no promises.

Is the vault live?

Not yet. The vault address is published in docs the moment it's on chain, with the payout tx list under it.
