How it works.

Paste a call

Any post on X that names a ticker. We read the post, the author and the view count live.

Launch on Pons

Bonding curve → Uniswap v4, liquidity locked by the PONS hook. The vault is the fee recipient.

Price it

Close on the day of the post vs price now, from Yahoo Finance. Direction decides the sign.

Friday settles

The best call of the week pays its holders from the vault. Pro rata, on chain, in USDG.

  1. I

    One coin per post. The first launch on a post is the coin; later ones are not listed.

  2. II

    Prices are read from the market, never entered. A deleted post scores zero.

  3. III

    The vault only holds fee income. It never buys, never sells, never holds coins.

  4. IV

    The caller can claim the launcher share any time by replying with their claim code. Until then it accrues to the vault.

Questions, answered.

What counts as a call?

Any public post on X that names a ticker — a stock or a coin — with a direction. "$TSLA long", "$SOL 300", "short this". You pick the asset and the direction when you register; the post is the receipt.

How is "since call" computed?

The asset's close on the day the post was published versus its price right now, both from Yahoo Finance, sign flipped for shorts. Memecoins with no history show price now only.

Does the caller have to agree?

No. It's a coin about a public call. The caller can claim the launcher's share any time by replying with their claim code, and profit from being right.

Where does the money come from?

Only from the 1% trade fee on Pons. 0.5% to the vault, 0.2% to the launcher, 0.3% to Pons. No emissions, no treasury, no promises.

Who gets paid?

Friday 20:00 UTC, the holders of the call coin with the largest gain in its direction that week — pro rata by balance at the snapshot, in USDG.

Is the vault live?

Not yet. The vault address is published in docs the moment it's on chain, with the payout tx list under it.
